MADA Progress Hub

Opening the hub… Open it here if nothing happens.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen, then Add.

The MADA icon opens your hub. Not in Safari or Chrome? Open mada.mprstudios.com/progress/?add=1 there first.
